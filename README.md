# ☀️ Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Blueprint for Renewable Energy

## 📋 Executive Summary
This repository houses the enterprise architecture, business process reengineering, and digital transformation blueprint tailored specifically for companies specializing in **Photovoltaic (PV) Solar** and **Aerothermal Energy** installations. 

Designed to bridge complex technical field operations with high-impact corporate economics, this framework standardizes the lifecycle from lead generation and engineering design to procurement, installation, and post-sales maintenance.

---

## 🏗️ Core Modules of the Renewable ERP

1. **CRM & Lead-to-Design Pipeline:**
   * Automated solar/aerothermal sizing estimations.
   * Integration with satellite mapping and geographic data for roof/property analysis.
2. **Supply Chain & Inventory Management:**
   * Specialized tracking for inverters, solar panels, heat pumps, mounting structures, and high-value components.
   * Just-in-time (JIT) procurement for installation schedules.
3. **Project Management & Field Operations:**
   * Workforce allocation (certified installers, electricians, plumbers).
   * Compliance tracking with local regulatory bodies, subsidies, and grid-connection permits.
4. **Financial Control & Economics:**
   * ROI calculators for clients.
   * Automated billing aligned with milestone-based installations.
   * Cash-flow forecasting for multi-stage green energy projects.

---

## 🔒 Governance & Security Posture
* **Zero-Trust Frameworks** for cloud-hosted customer data and energy grid integrations.
* Compliance with international quality and project management standards (PMP®, Six Sigma).
